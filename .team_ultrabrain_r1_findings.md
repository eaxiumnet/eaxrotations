## 🔴 CRITICAL #10: Interrupt Manager Missing School Lock Tracking (Agent-Discovered)

`EaxRotations/shared/interrupt_manager_sylvanas.lua` — **full file**

The interrupt manager tracks casting state and adds humanization jitter, but **never records which spell school was locked after a successful interrupt.**

In TBC:
- Counterspell: locks the spell's school for 2s (4s w/ Improved Counterspell)
- Pummel/Kick/Earth Shock: locks for 2s (physical for casters, irrelevant for melee)
- Spell Lock: locks for 3s
- Silencing Shot: silences for 3s

**Impact**: After a successful interrupt, the rotation cannot:
- Know whether Counterspell is still active on the target
- Optimize follow-up casts during a known lock window
- Avoid wasting secondary interrupts on already-locked casts

**Recommendation**: Add `record_school_lock(target, spell_id, duration)` and expose `is_school_locked(target, school)` / `school_lock_remains(target, school)`.
