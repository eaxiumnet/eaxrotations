# Paladin Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Paladin spec guides, Wowhead Paladin ability database, Wowhead Paladin talent calculator, Warcraft Tavern seals guide, Flux Paladin research, Sonah Paladin modules, SlyRotate Paladin module.

## Class Kit

- Seals and Judgement are the class core. In TBC, one Judgement spell unleashes the active seal.
- Blessings: Might, Wisdom, Kings, Salvation, Sanctuary, Light, Protection, Freedom, Sacrifice.
- Auras: Devotion, Retribution, Concentration, resistance auras, Sanctity, Crusader.
- Utility/defense: Cleanse, Purify, Hammer of Justice, Divine Shield, Divine Protection, Lay on Hands, Righteous Defense, Righteous Fury.
- TBC additions: Crusader Strike, Avenger's Shield, Seal of Blood/Vengeance/Martyr behavior, Avenging Wrath, Spiritual Attunement, Righteous Defense, Divine Illumination.
- Do not add Wrath+ mechanics: Divine Storm, Beacon of Light, Sacred Shield, Divine Plea, Hand renames, Holy Power.

## Talent Trees

- Holy: Holy Shock, Divine Favor, Divine Illumination, Light's Grace, Illumination mana returns.
- Protection: Righteous Fury threat, Holy Shield, Avenger's Shield, Blessing of Sanctuary, defense/block scaling.
- Retribution: Seal of Command, Sanctity Aura, Vengeance, Crusader Strike, Fanaticism, seal twisting.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Holy: Flash of Light, Holy Light, Holy Shock, Divine Favor, Divine Illumination, Cleanse.
- Protection: Righteous Fury, Holy Shield, Consecration, Judgement, Seal of Righteousness/Wisdom/Vengeance, Avenger's Shield on pull.
- Retribution: Seal of Command, Seal of Blood or Seal of the Martyr, Judgement, Crusader Strike, Consecration, Exorcism versus Undead/Demon, Hammer of Wrath execute.
