# Rogue Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Rogue guide, Wowhead Rogue ability database, Wowhead Rogue talent calculator, Flux Rogue research, Sonah Rogue modules, SlyRotate Rogue module.

## Class Kit

- Builders: Sinister Strike, Backstab, Hemorrhage, Mutilate if available in the TBC ruleset being targeted, Shiv, Gouge.
- Finishers: Slice and Dice, Rupture, Eviscerate, Expose Armor, Kidney Shot, Envenom where supported.
- Cooldowns: Blade Flurry, Adrenaline Rush, Cold Blood, Preparation, Shadowstep, Evasion, Sprint, Vanish, Cloak of Shadows.
- Utility/control: Kick, Cheap Shot, Garrote, Sap, Blind, Distract, Crippling/Wound/Mind-numbing poisons.
- TBC additions: Cloak of Shadows, Shiv, Deadly Throw, Envenom, Shadowstep.

## Talent Trees

- Assassination: poison/crit/burst tools, Cold Blood, Mutilate depending on ruleset and weapon constraints.
- Combat: strongest PvE raid build in most TBC contexts, driven by weapon specialization, Blade Flurry, Adrenaline Rush, Sinister Strike.
- Subtlety: PvP/control tree with Hemorrhage, Preparation, Shadowstep, opener strength.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- PvE baseline: maintain Slice and Dice, maintain Rupture if target lives long enough, use Sinister Strike/Backstab/Hemorrhage as builder, cooldown stack Blade Flurry/Adrenaline Rush.
- Armor debuff role: Improved Expose Armor can replace Sunder Armor only when assigned; avoid overwriting stronger raid debuff plans.
- Poisons: maintain weapon poisons; PvP swaps matter for Wound, Crippling, Mind-numbing, and Shiv.
