# Druid Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Druid spec guides, Wowhead Druid ability database, Wowhead Druid talent calculator, Flux Druid research, Sonah Druid modules, SlyRotate Feral Druid module.

## Class Kit

- Forms: Bear/Dire Bear for tanking, Cat for melee DPS, Moonkin for Balance, Tree of Life for Restoration, Travel/Aquatic/Flight for movement.
- Core utility: Mark/Gift of the Wild, Thorns, Innervate, Rebirth, Barkskin, Remove Curse, Abolish Poison, Hibernate, Entangling Roots, Cyclone, Faerie Fire.
- TBC additions to preserve: Mangle, Lacerate, Lifebloom, Force of Nature, Tree of Life, Cyclone, Maim, Flight Form.
- Do not add Wrath+ mechanics: Wild Growth, Savage Roar, Berserk, Survival Instincts, Nourish, Typhoon, Starfall, Eclipse.

## Talent Trees

- Balance: caster DPS and raid support through Moonkin Form, Improved Faerie Fire, Nature's Grace, Insect Swarm, Force of Nature.
- Feral: Cat DPS and Bear tanking share most talents; Mangle is the defining deep talent, while Furor/Omen of Clarity support powershifting and efficient attacks.
- Restoration: HoT healing, Nature's Swiftness, Swiftmend, Tree of Life, and Lifebloom rolling.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Cat: Mangle (Cat), Shred, Rake, Rip, Ferocious Bite, Tiger's Fury, Maim, Faerie Fire (Feral), powershift via Cat Form.
- Bear: Mangle (Bear), Lacerate, Maul, Swipe, Demoralizing Roar, Faerie Fire (Feral), Enrage, Frenzied Regeneration, Growl, Challenging Roar.
- Balance: Starfire, Wrath, Moonfire, Insect Swarm, Hurricane, Force of Nature, Faerie Fire.
- Restoration: Lifebloom, Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Healing Touch, Swiftmend, Nature's Swiftness, Tranquility.
