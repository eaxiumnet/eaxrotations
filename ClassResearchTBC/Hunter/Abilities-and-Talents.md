# Hunter Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Hunter spec guides, Wowhead Hunter ability database, Wowhead Hunter talent calculator, Flux Hunter research, Sonah Hunter modules, SlyRotate Hunter module.

## Class Kit

- Ranged attacks: Auto Shot, Steady Shot, Multi-Shot, Arcane Shot, Aimed Shot, Kill Command, Volley.
- Stings: Serpent Sting, Scorpid Sting, Viper Sting, Wyvern Sting for Survival.
- Traps: Freezing, Frost, Immolation, Explosive, Snake. Trap use is highly positional and often PvP/control driven.
- Aspects: Hawk for damage, Viper for mana recovery, Cheetah/Pack for travel, Monkey for defense.
- Pet management: pet attack, focus dump abilities, Mend Pet, Bestial Wrath, Intimidation, Frenzy/Ferocious Inspiration support depending on spec.
- TBC additions: Steady Shot, Kill Command, Misdirection, Aspect of the Viper, Snake Trap.

## Talent Trees

- Beast Mastery: highest sustained PvE damage through pet scaling, Bestial Wrath, Serpent's Swiftness, Ferocious Inspiration.
- Marksmanship: Trueshot Aura, Aimed Shot, Readiness-style cooldown value, stronger direct shots.
- Survival: Expose Weakness raid utility, Wyvern Sting, strong agility scaling, optional melee-weaving complexity.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- All specs: Auto Shot timing is mandatory; Steady Shot must be woven without clipping Auto Shot.
- BM: Bestial Wrath, Kill Command, Steady Shot rhythm, pet uptime.
- MM: Trueshot Aura, Aimed Shot, Multi-Shot, Arcane Shot, Steady Shot.
- Survival: Expose Weakness uptime from crits, Serpent Sting maintenance, traps when in range, Steady Shot filler.
