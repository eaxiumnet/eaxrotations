# Shaman Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Shaman spec guides, Wowhead Shaman ability database, Wowhead Shaman talent calculator, Warcraft Wiki Totem Twisting, Flux Shaman research, Sonah Shaman modules, SlyRotate Shaman module.

## Class Kit

- Shocks: Earth Shock, Flame Shock, Frost Shock share a cooldown. Earth Shock is the TBC interrupt.
- Totems: one Fire, Earth, Water, and Air totem active at a time; no Totemic Recall or multi-totem drop in TBC.
- Shields: Water Shield, Lightning Shield, Earth Shield if Restoration. Shields are mutually exclusive where applicable.
- Weapon imbues: Windfury, Flametongue, Frostbrand, Rockbiter.
- Utility: Purge, Cure Poison, Cure Disease, Tremor Totem, Grounding Totem, Earthbind Totem, Bloodlust/Heroism, Reincarnation.
- TBC additions: Water Shield, Earth Shield, Totem of Wrath, Wrath of Air, Earth/Fire Elemental Totems, Bloodlust/Heroism, Shamanistic Rage, Dual Wield.
- Do not add Wrath+ mechanics: Wind Shear, Lava Burst, Riptide, Hex, Thunderstorm, Feral Spirit, Maelstrom Weapon, Lava Lash, direct Fire Nova.

## Talent Trees

- Elemental: Lightning Bolt/Chain Lightning caster with Elemental Mastery, Lightning Overload, Totem of Wrath.
- Enhancement: dual-wield melee with Stormstrike, Shamanistic Rage, Flurry, Unleashed Rage, totem twisting.
- Restoration: Chain Heal, Earth Shield, Mana Tide Totem, Nature's Swiftness, efficient raid healing.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Elemental: Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Flame Shock, Earth Shock, Elemental Mastery, Totem of Wrath.
- Enhancement: Stormstrike, Earth/Flame/Frost Shock choice, Shamanistic Rage, Windfury Weapon, Flametongue Weapon, Windfury/Grace of Air totem twist.
- Restoration: Earth Shield, Chain Heal, Lesser Healing Wave, Healing Wave, Nature's Swiftness, Mana Tide Totem.
