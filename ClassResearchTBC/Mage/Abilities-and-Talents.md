# Mage Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Mage spec guides, Wowhead Mage ability database, Wowhead Mage talent calculator, Flux Mage research, Sonah Mage modules, SlyRotate Mage module.

## Class Kit

- Damage schools: Arcane, Fire, Frost.
- Core utility: Arcane Intellect/Brilliance, Remove Lesser Curse, Polymorph, Counterspell, Blink, Ice Block, Ice Barrier if Frost, Evocation, Mana Gem, Spellsteal in TBC.
- AoE: Arcane Explosion, Flamestrike, Blizzard, Cone of Cold, Blast Wave if Fire.
- TBC additions to preserve: Arcane Blast, Spellsteal, Invisibility, Icy Veins, Water Elemental.
- Do not add Wrath+ mechanics such as Brain Freeze, Frostfire Bolt, Deep Freeze, or Living Bomb.

## Talent Trees

- Arcane: Arcane Blast mana-stack gameplay, Arcane Power, Presence of Mind, strong burst with heavy mana management.
- Fire: Ignite and Improved Scorch support, Combustion cooldown, Fireball filler, high threat sensitivity.
- Frost: Frostbolt sustained filler, Icy Veins, Water Elemental, superior control and survivability.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Arcane: Arcane Blast stacks, Arcane Missiles/Fireball/Frostbolt conserve options depending on build, Arcane Power, Presence of Mind, Evocation, Mana Gem.
- Fire: Scorch to build/maintain Improved Scorch, Fireball filler, Combustion, Fire Blast movement/filler, Blast Wave/Flamestrike AoE.
- Frost: Frostbolt filler, Icy Veins, Summon Water Elemental, Cold Snap, Blizzard/Cone of Cold AoE/control.
