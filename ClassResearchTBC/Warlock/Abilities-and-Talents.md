# Warlock Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Warlock spec guides, Wowhead Warlock ability database, Wowhead Warlock talent calculator, Flux Warlock research, Sonah Warlock modules, SlyRotate Warlock module.

## Class Kit

- DoTs: Corruption, Curse choices, Immolate, Siphon Life, Unstable Affliction.
- Nukes: Shadow Bolt, Incinerate, Searing Pain, Soul Fire, Shadowburn.
- AoE: Seed of Corruption, Rain of Fire, Hellfire, Shadowfury if Destruction.
- Mana engine: Life Tap, Dark Pact if Affliction.
- Pets: Imp, Voidwalker, Succubus, Felhunter, Felguard if Demonology; pet choice affects utility and sacrifice buffs.
- Utility: Fear, Howl of Terror, Death Coil, Banish, Enslave Demon, Create Healthstone/Soulstone, Curse assignment.
- TBC additions: Seed of Corruption, Unstable Affliction, Felguard, Incinerate, Soulshatter, Shadowfury.

## Talent Trees

- Affliction: DoT maintenance, Shadow Embrace, Unstable Affliction, Siphon Life, Dark Pact.
- Demonology: pet scaling, Felguard, Demonic Sacrifice variants, survivability.
- Destruction: Shadow Bolt or Fire/Incinerate build, Ruin, Shadowfury, Conflagrate/Immolate interaction.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Affliction: assigned Curse, Unstable Affliction, Corruption, Siphon Life, Immolate depending on build, Shadow Bolt filler, Life Tap/Dark Pact.
- Demonology: maintain key DoTs if worth it, Shadow Bolt filler, Felguard uptime or Demonic Sacrifice buff.
- Destruction: Shadow Bolt spam for shadow build; Immolate plus Incinerate for fire build; Conflagrate consumes Immolate in TBC and must be gated.
- AoE: Seed of Corruption is primary multi-target spell when enemies live long enough.
