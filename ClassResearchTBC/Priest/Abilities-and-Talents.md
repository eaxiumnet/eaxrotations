# Priest Abilities and Talents

Sources: `../Sources.md`, `../LocalReferences.md`, Icy Veins Priest guides, Wowhead Priest ability database, Wowhead Priest talent calculator, Flux Priest research, Sonah Priest modules, SlyRotate Priest module.

## Class Kit

- Healing: Flash Heal, Greater Heal, Heal, Renew, Prayer of Healing, Binding Heal, Prayer of Mending, Circle of Healing if Holy.
- Absorbs/defense: Power Word: Shield, Inner Fire, Fear Ward if Dwarf/Draenei, Pain Suppression if Discipline.
- Damage: Smite, Holy Fire, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, Mind Flay, Vampiric Touch, Shadow Word: Death.
- Utility: Dispel Magic, Cure Disease, Mass Dispel, Psychic Scream, Mind Control, Shackle Undead, Power Word: Fortitude/Prayer of Fortitude, Shadow Protection.
- TBC additions: Prayer of Mending, Binding Heal, Mass Dispel, Shadowfiend, Vampiric Touch, Pain Suppression, Circle of Healing.

## Talent Trees

- Discipline: Power Infusion, Pain Suppression, Meditation, Improved Power Word: Shield, PvP durability and utility.
- Holy: Spiritual Healing, Holy Concentration, Circle of Healing, improved throughput and AoE healing.
- Shadow: Shadowform, Vampiric Touch, Shadow Weaving, Misery, Vampiric Embrace support.

## Rotation-Impacting Abilities

- Discipline/Holy healing: Prayer of Mending, Renew, Flash Heal, Greater Heal, Binding Heal, Prayer of Healing, Circle of Healing, Power Word: Shield with Weakened Soul checks.
- Shadow: Vampiric Touch, Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Death, Mind Flay, Shadowfiend.
- Smite: Holy Fire, Smite, Shadow Word: Pain optional, Inner Focus, Power Infusion if talented.
