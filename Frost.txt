Full Shadow Priest DPS automation for TBC Classic (level 70). Handles the complete priority rotation, DoT uptime across multiple targets, mana cooldowns, and situational defensives — all in a standalone plugin.



Key Features:



Priority Rotation - Vampiric Touch → Shadow Word: Pain → Mind Blast → Shadow Word: Death (execute) → Mind Flay filler. Follows the standard TBC shadow priority with correct cooldown and talent checks.



Multi-DoT Engine - SW:P and Vampiric Touch automatically spread to nearby enemies in Cleave and AoE modes. Configurable target count and range. Per-target lockout tracking prevents double-queuing while a DoT cast is in flight.



Combat Mode Selection - Switch between Auto (detects enemy count), Single Target, Cleave, and AoE directly from the Status HUD or GUI without opening menus mid-pull.



Mana Management - Shadowfiend fires automatically when mana drops below a configurable threshold. Inner Focus is held for Mind Blast to maximize the free + 25% crit bonus.



Self-Buffs - Shadowform and Inner Fire are maintained automatically out of combat and re-applied mid-combat if they drop.



Defensive Chain - Power Word: Shield, Flash Heal self-heal, and Psychic Scream are all HP-gated and independently toggleable.



Interrupts - Silence fires automatically on interruptible casts when you have the 15-pt Shadow talent trained.



Configuration Options:



Combat Mode: Auto / Single Target / Cleave / AoE

Multi-DoT Mode: All in Range / Near Target / Off

Shadowfiend Mana Threshold: 10–80%

SW:D Min Self Health: 20–100% (backlash safety gate)

DoT Refresh Windows: configurable per-DoT remaining duration



Perfect For:



Shadow Priests running dungeons, raids, or open-world content who want consistent DoT uptime and mana efficiency without manual Shadowfiend timing.



Rotation Priority:



1. Silence (interrupt, when trained)

2. Vampiric Touch (maintain, 15s DoT)

3. Shadow Word: Pain (maintain, 18s DoT)

4. Shadow Word: Death (below HP threshold)

5. Inner Focus → Mind Blast

6. Mind Flay (channeled filler)



Advanced Features:



Multi-DoT Spread - DoTs tracked per-target GUID with independent refresh timers and pending/lockout markers to prevent cast overlap.

TTD Gate - DoTs are not reapplied to targets about to die.

Mounted Bail - No casts or buff maintenance while mounted.

A fully automated healer rotation for TBC Classic covering both Holy and Discipline. Spec is auto-detected at load using signature spells — no manual switching ever needed. If you respec mid-session the plugin switches over within ~30 seconds. Built and actively maintained in collaboration with MyTakeX.



Key Features:



Auto Spec Detection - Detects Holy vs. Discipline automatically using signature spells (Prayer of Mending → Holy, Power Infusion → Discipline). Switches live on respec.

Stop-Cast Engine - Cancels a heal mid-cast when the target recovers above threshold, using multi-point HP checkpoints to avoid overheal waste on long casts like Greater Heal.

Pre-Heal System - Queues a Greater Heal or Flash Heal proactively based on incoming damage patterns. Mana-gated and tunable via HP thresholds.

Tank-Priority Target Selection - Tanks and focus-target receive a configurable HP bias so they are always picked first when HP is close.

Per-Spell HP Thresholds - Every spell has its own tunable HP% trigger: Renew, Prayer of Healing, Greater Heal, Binding Heal, Flash Heal, and Circle of Healing each fire independently.

Binding Heal Self-Deprioritization - When Binding Heal is on, self HP is treated as inflated for target selection, so the rotation prefers tanks/DPS first and lets Binding Heal catch you up for free.

Dispel Magic - Fully automated on party members.

Fade - Auto-cast for aggro drop when you are targeted and a backup healer exists in range.

Healthstone - Used automatically below a configurable HP threshold.



Configuration Options:



Healing Threshold: Global HP% below which any heal is considered

Tank Focus Mode: On / Off — locks target priority to focus target only

Power Word: Shield: On / Off, tank-only mode available

Prayer of Mending: On / Off, tank priority or lowest-HP mode

Circle of Healing: Holy-only, configurable target count minimum and HP%

Prayer of Healing: On / Off, configurable min targets and HP%

Stop-Casting: On / Off, four HP checkpoint sliders

Pre-Healing: On / Off, spell choice (Greater Heal / Flash Heal), mana floor, emergency HP

Inner Focus: On / Off

Healthstone: On / Off, HP% threshold



Perfect For:



Players who want a hands-off healer that intelligently juggles throughput and overheal prevention. Equally at home in dungeons, raids, and leveling group content. Works for both Holy and Discipline without ever touching a config.



Rotation Priority:



1. Healthstone (self-survival below threshold)

2. Fade (aggro drop when targeted, backup healer present)

3. Inner Focus (free next expensive heal)

4. Dispel Magic

5. Power Word: Shield (tank / lowest HP, gated by Weakened Soul)

6. Prayer of Mending (tank or lowest HP, kept on cooldown)

7. Renew (HoT maintenance below threshold)

8. Circle of Healing (Holy only — min targets below CoH HP%)

9. Prayer of Healing (min targets below PoH HP%)

10. Greater Heal (pushback-protected, below GH HP%)

11. Binding Heal (self + target both below BH HP%)

12. Flash Heal (emergency below FH HP%)



Advanced Features:



Encounter Reactions - Built-in handling for Netherspite Dominance Beam, Shadow Grasp (Maiden), and Garrote (Moroes) via the encounters module.

FB Nexus Integration - Session guard prevents double-loads on /reload; cast wrapper and predictive modules hook directly into Nexus infrastructure.

GUID Crash Fix
v1.2.0
01/31/2026 05:02
Fixed "Invalid game object!" crash on line 1085

The crash occurred when comparing target GUIDs during auto-attack tracking. When a target despawns (dies, phases out, etc.), the GUID object becomes stale. Comparing a stale GUID with the ~= operator triggers an object access, causing the crash.

The fix converts GUIDs to strings immediately, so we're comparing safe immutable values instead of game object references.
