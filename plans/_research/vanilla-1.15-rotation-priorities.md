## DPS / TANK SPECS

### 1. Priest Shadow

**Priority List (single-target raid):**
1. Maintain 5 stacks of **Shadow Weaving** on the boss (passive from Shadow spells)
2. Keep **Shadow Word: Pain** active (max rank; Rank 1 to refresh if moving/low mana)
3. **Mind Blast** on cooldown
4. **Mind Flay** as filler

**Key Spells:** Shadow Word: Pain, Mind Blast, Mind Flay, Shadow Weaving, Inner Focus (used with Mind Blast), Shadowform

**Differences from TBC:**
- No Vampiric Touch (TBC)
- Only one Shadow Priest is brought to 40-man raids primarily for Shadow Weaving (Warlock buff bot)
- Vampiric Embrace and Devouring Plague are **NOT** used on raid bosses because they consume precious debuff slots

**Consensus:** Near-unanimous across Icy Veins and Wowhead. Rotation is deliberately simple due to debuff slot constraints.

**Source citations:**
- Icy Veins: "Shadow Word: Pain (Rank 1) can be used to keep up Shadow Weaving... Keeping this buff up is vital for your own damage and the warlocks in your raid."
- Wowhead: "Shadow Priests were mostly brought to raids to buff the damage of your Warlocks through Shadow Weaving."

### 2. Priest Smite / Holy DPS

**Priority List (raid boss):**
1. Pre-pull: proc weapon buff (e.g., Hand of Edward the Odd)
2. **Shadow Word: Pain** on pull (if raid leader allows the debuff slot)
3. **Mind Blast** on cooldown (once debuffs/trinkets are active)
4. **Smite** as primary filler (cast constantly)
5. If **Seal of the Crusader** is active on the boss, skip Mind Blast and cast Smite only

**Key Spells:** Smite, Mind Blast, Shadow Word: Pain, Holy Fire (often precast/off-CD), Inner Focus, Power Infusion (self or friendly DPS)

**Differences from TBC:**
- This is a Vanilla-niche build. It relies on Holy damage (unresisted) and Paladin debuffs.
- Power Infusion comes from the Discipline tree; typical build is ~20/31/0
- Requires Sanctity Aura + Improved Seal of the Crusader from Paladin

**Consensus:** Warcraft Tavern and Barrens Chat agree on the Smite + Mind Blast weave. Source notes: "if Seal of the Crusader is on the boss, cast non-stop Smite instead."

**Source citations:**
- Warcraft Tavern: "The moment all debuffs are on the boss, activate your spell damage trinket, cast Inner Focus, cast Power Infusion on yourself and cast Mind Blast each time its off cooldown. (BUT if Seal of the Crusader is on the boss cast non-stop Smite instead.)"

### 3. Shaman Elemental

**Single-Target Priority:**
1. **Lesser Healing Wave** (emergency save)
2. Utility: purge dangerous debuffs, **Tremor Totem** for Fear
3. Keep group buff totems + **Searing Totem** active
4. **Earth Shock** to execute low-health enemies
5. **Chain Lightning** during Elemental Focus (Clearcasting) proc
6. **Lightning Bolt** (main filler; downrank to Rank 4 for long fights)

**Multi-Target (3+):** Replace Lightning Bolt with Chain Lightning. Use **Fire Nova Totem** if in melee range.

**Key Spells:** Lightning Bolt, Chain Lightning, Earth Shock, Searing Totem, Tremor Totem, Elemental Focus

**Differences from TBC:**
- No Lava Burst (Wrath)
- No dual-wield or Shamanistic Rage (TBC Enhancement)
- Downranking Lightning Bolt to Rank 4 is essential for mana efficiency (Rank 3 is learned at level 14 and gets penalized spell coefficient)

**Consensus:** Icy Veins and Wowhead are identical. Priority is well-established.

**Source citations:**
- Icy Veins: "Rank 4 Lightning Bolt gets the same benefit from Spell Damage as Rank 10, but is much cheaper."

### 4. Shaman Enhancement

**Solo / Small Group Priority:**
1. **Stormstrike** on cooldown
2. Rotate **Flame Shock** and **Earth Shock** on cooldown (keep Flame Shock DoT running)
3. Keep totems up (Searing, Windfury, etc.)

**Raid Priority:**
1. Stormstrike on cooldown
2. **Frost Shock** on cooldown (unless Totem Twisting, then save mana)
3. Keep totems up
4. Often assigned to wield **Nightfall** or **Annihilator** for raid debuff uptime

**Multi-Target:** Fire Nova Totem, Magma Totem

**Key Spells:** Stormstrike, Earth Shock, Frost Shock, Flame Shock, Windfury Weapon, Windfury Totem

**Differences from TBC:**
- No Maelstrom Weapon (Wrath)
- No Feral Spirit/Spirit Wolves (Wrath)
- No dual-wield specialization
- In raids, Enhancement is primarily a buff bot / Nightfall wielder with a very passive rotation

**Consensus:** Icy Veins splits Solo vs Raid. Wowhead confirms the "buff bot" raid role. Totem Twisting (Windfury + Grace of Air) is an advanced Vanilla technique.

**Source citations:**
- Wowhead: "Because of their lower personal damage contribution, Enhancement Shaman are typically the go-to specialization for maintaining important raid debuffs such as Annihilator and Nightfall."

### 5. Druid Balance

**Priority List:**
1. **Starfire** -- cast from start to finish (only spell used in most raids)
2. Downrank Starfire (Ranks 4-6) for mana conservation
3. **Hurricane** for heavy AoE pulls only

**What NOT to cast in raids:** Moonfire and Insect Swarm (waste debuff slots for weak damage). Wrath is less mana-efficient and weaker than Starfire.

**Key Spells:** Starfire, Hurricane, Innervate (usually given to a healer), Moonkin Form

**Differences from TBC:**
- No Eclipse mechanic (Wrath)
- No Force of Nature treants (TBC talent, but even in TBC they were weak)
- Rotation is famously "one button" in Vanilla

**Consensus:** Icy Veins is explicit: "Balance Druids do not have any depth to their rotation. You will cast Starfire as many times as possible."

**Source citations:**
- Icy Veins: "You cannot use [Moonfire/Insect Swarm] for a few reasons. First, Moonfire and Insect Swarm are damage over time spells that take up a debuff slot on the boss, of which only 16 are available for the whole raid."

### 6. Druid Feral Cat

**Priority List:**
1. **Powershift** (Wolfshead Helm + Furor talent) to generate 60 Energy per shift
2. **Shred** (primary combo point builder)
3. Maintain **Rip**
4. **Ferocious Bite** (finisher; optimal rotation uses both Rip and FB)
5. **Claw** (if unable to Shred from behind)

**Key Spells:** Shred, Rip, Ferocious Bite, Powershift macro, Cat Form, Tiger's Fury, Manual Crowd Pummeler (consumable weapon for haste)

**Differences from TBC:**
- **No Mangle** (TBC)
- **No Savage Roar** (Wrath)
- **No Berserk** (Wrath)
- Powershifting is the defining Vanilla mechanic that makes the spec competitive

**Consensus:** Wowhead guide is extremely detailed on powershifting cycles. Community theorycraft is still evolving, but the Shred -> Rip/FB priority is agreed upon.

**Source citations:**
- Wowhead: "It was just recently discovered that unlike the first iteration of WoW Classic, the optimal Feral rotation in WoW Classic will actually use a combination of both Rip and Ferocious Bite as finishing moves for dumping Combo Points."

### 7. Druid Feral Bear (Tank)

**Priority List:**
1. **Maul** -- queue on every single auto-attack (highest threat, consumes Rage)
2. **Swipe** -- multi-target or on pull for snap threat
3. **Faerie Fire (Feral)** -- apply for armor reduction
4. **Demoralizing Roar** -- reduce enemy AP
5. **Growl** / **Challenging Roar** -- taunts as needed
6. **Barkskin** (1 min CD, 20% reduction) for magic/high damage phases
7. **Frenzied Regeneration** -- Rage-to-health conversion when low

**Key Spells:** Maul, Swipe, Growl, Faerie Fire (Feral), Demoralizing Roar, Barkskin, Frenzied Regeneration

**Differences from TBC:**
- **No Lacerate** (TBC)
- **No Mangle** (TBC)
- Threat is almost entirely from Maul
- Manual Crowd Pummeler (Gnomeregan mace) is essential for 50% attack speed threat windows

**Consensus:** Icy Veins and Wowhead agree: "Maul is your highest threat ability." Bears cannot block or parry; they scale armor and stamina.

**Source citations:**
- Icy Veins: "You should almost never get an actual pure auto attack off since every single attack should be affected by Maul."

## HEALER SPECS

### 8. Priest Discipline

**Vanilla Reality Check:** Discipline is **NOT** a primary PvE healing spec in Classic 1.15.x. It is a PvP spec or a "Power Infusion bot" sub-spec (typically 31/20/0) that sacrifices healing throughput for **Power Infusion** (20% spell damage/healing on a friendly target for 15 sec).

**If playing Discipline in a PvE setting:**
1. **Power Word: Shield** -- emergency mitigation (inefficient mana-wise)
2. **Greater Heal** -- tank healing (slower than Holy due to missing Holy talents)
3. **Flash Heal** -- emergency spot heal
4. **Renew** -- HoT while moving
5. **Power Infusion** -- cast on your best Mage/Warlock on cooldown

**Key Spells:** Power Infusion, Inner Focus, PW: Shield, Greater Heal, Flash Heal, Renew

**Differences from TBC/Wrath:**
- **No Penance** (Wrath)
- **No Atonement** (Cata)
- **No Divine Aegis** (Wrath)
- **No Rapture** (Wrath)
- Discipline in Vanilla is basically "Holy-lite + Power Infusion"

**Consensus:** Split -- most guides (Icy Veins, NoobToBoss) treat Discipline as a PvP/utility spec, not a raid healer. Deep Holy is preferred for all PvE healing.

### 9. Priest Holy

**Tank Healing Priority:**
1. **Renew** on tank (if constant damage)
2. **Greater Heal** -- primary tank heal (Ranks 1, 3, 5 for downranking)
3. **Flash Heal** -- emergencies only (expensive)
4. **PW: Shield** -- predictable burst or save-from-death
5. **Heal** (Rank 3) -- low damage periods, mana conservation

**Raid Healing Priority:**
1. **Prayer of Healing** -- 3+ injured players in your party
2. **Flash Heal** -- critical health targets
3. **Greater Heal** -- moderate single-target damage
4. **Renew** -- while moving or for constant chip damage

**Key Spells:** Greater Heal, Flash Heal, Heal, Renew, Prayer of Healing, PW: Shield, Inner Focus

**Differences from TBC:**
- No Circle of Healing (TBC)
- No Binding Heal (TBC)
- No Prayer of Mending (TBC)
- Downranking is essential

**Consensus:** NoobToBoss and Icy Veins are nearly identical. Downranking Greater Heal to Rank 1/3 is core to mana management.

**Source citations:**
- NoobToBoss: "Greater Heal: Your most efficient heal per cast time. Use this as your primary tank healing spell."
- Icy Veins: "Use Greater Heal whenever you know that a large amount of damage is incoming. Try and time for the cast to finish just as the damage happens."

### 10. Shaman Restoration

**Priority List:**
1. **Lesser Healing Wave** -- emergency "save from death"
2. Remove dangerous debuffs / **Tremor Totem** for Fear
3. Keep group-buffing totems active (Windfury, Mana Spring, etc.)
4. Maintain **Ancestral Healing** (+25% armor) and **Healing Way** (if tank healing) on tank
5. **Chain Heal** -- primary raid heal (often downranked to Rank 1 for mana efficiency)
6. **Healing Wave** -- single-target if assigned to tank (with Healing Way stacks)

**Key Spells:** Chain Heal, Healing Wave, Lesser Healing Wave, Ancestral Healing, Mana Tide Totem, Windfury Totem

**Differences from TBC:**
- **No Riptide** (Wrath)
- **No Earth Shield** (TBC)
- **No Water Shield** (TBC)
- Chain Heal Rank 1 spam is iconic Vanilla raid healing

**Consensus:** Icy Veins and Warcraft Tavern agree: "You'll actually do more healing doing nothing but casting the lowest rank of Chain Heal all fight." Resto Shamans are raid healers; tank healing drains mana too fast without Healing Way.

**Source citations:**
- Warcraft Tavern: "To put things into perspective, you'll actually do more healing doing nothing but casting the lowest rank of Chain Heal all fight than you would be by trying to use Healing Wave at all."

### 11. Druid Restoration

**Tank Healing Priority:**
1. Keep **Rejuvenation** and **Regrowth** rolling on the tank
2. **Healing Touch** or **Regrowth** for direct healing (depends on build)
3. **Swiftmend** (if specced) -- emergency burst heal consuming a HoT
4. **Rank 4 Healing Touch** -- the single most mana-efficient heal in the game for low damage

**Raid Healing Priority:**
1. **Rejuvenation** -- preemptively place on raid members before damage
2. **Regrowth** -- if using the Regrowth build (with Improved Regrowth + Nature's Grace)
3. **Healing Touch** -- if using the Moonglow build (mana-efficient direct heals)
4. **Tranquility** -- raid cooldown for heavy group damage

**Key Spells:** Rejuvenation, Regrowth, Healing Touch (Rank 4), Swiftmend, Tranquility, Innervate, Rebirth

**Differences from TBC:**
- **No Lifebloom** (TBC)
- **No Wild Growth** (Wrath)
- **No Nourish** (Wrath)
- Swiftmend is a talent in Vanilla (Restoration tier), not baseline

**Consensus:** Icy Veins is clear: HoT rolling on the tank is critical. Rank 4 Healing Touch is the most mana-efficient heal in Vanilla. Build choice (Swiftmend vs Moonglow) determines whether you favor Regrowth or Healing Touch.

**Source citations:**
- Icy Veins: "Rank 4 of Healing Touch is the single most Mana-efficient heal in the game, making it a must-have on your bars for low Mana consumption healing."

## KEY VANILLA MECHANICS NOT IN TBC

1. **16 debuff slot limit** -- This shapes almost every DPS rotation. DoTs and debuffs are precious.
2. **Downranking** -- Using lower ranks of spells is essential for mana efficiency. Spell coefficient penalty for spells learned before level 20.
3. **5-Second Rule** -- Mana regen from Spirit only works if you haven't cast for 5 seconds.
4. **Powershifting** -- Feral Druids shift out/back into Cat Form to trigger Furor energy procs.
5. **Totem Twisting** -- Shamans cast Windfury then Grace of Air every 10 seconds to maintain both buffs.
6. **Manual Crowd Pummeler** -- Gnomeregan mace with 50% haste on-use is BiS for Feral DPS and Bear threat.
