# EAX Priest Discipline — v2.1.0

Discipline Priest healing automation for TBC on Project Sylvanas.

## Rotation
- `Power Word: Shield` maintained on tank and low HP targets.
- `Renew` for sustained HoT uptime.
- `Prayer of Mending` on cooldown (bouncing heal).
- `Flash Heal` for emergency direct heals.
- `Power Infusion` for self-burst throughput when healing pressure is present.
- `Pain Suppression` for emergency damage reduction.
- OOC: `Power Word: Fortitude` and `Divine Spirit` maintained.

## New in 2.1.0
- **Inner Fire:** Auto-maintained as a self-buff.

## Install
1. Extract `EAXPriestDiscipline` into your Sylvanas `scripts` folder.
2. Reload or restart.
3. Enable `EAX Priest Discipline` in the menu.
